Model Equations

dS/dt = aA + bT + cW - dR - eP

dE/dt = fR + gL - hS - iT - jW

dR/dt = kL + mP - nS - qT

Stress rises from academics, training, and work, then falls when recovery and support help buffer the load. Energy rises from recovery and sleep, while stress, training, and work drain it. Recovery grows with sleep and support, but high stress and hard training make it harder to rebuild.

Euler step: next value = current value + dt * rate of change.
